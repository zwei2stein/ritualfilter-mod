[h1]Ritual Filter[/h1]

When you are picking participants for rituals, it can be bothersome with huge colony. Some rituals (like anomaly or medal awards) kinda need that one specific pawn.

[h2]Features[/h2] 

This mod lets you to gray out/higlight pawns in ritual selection dialog to easily identify the ones you want participating in special slots.

[list]
    [*]Simple search - type part of pawns name
    [*]More complex searches - look for pawns with traits, passions, etc ...
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Search syntax[/h2]

Simple search is just typing part of pawns name. For example [b]bel[/b] looks for pawns that have "bel" in name, like Belle or Mirabel. Note that whole pawns name is searched, so pawns with weird surname can get highlighted.

You can do more complex searches ty chaining several statements with space. Space meaning "and". For example [b]passion:cooking age>18[/b] - this one highlights adult pawns with passion for cooking.

If something has space in name, like trait "Hard worker", you may use underscore [b]_[/b] to replace space. [b]trait:Hard worker[/b] would search for pawn with trait "Hard" and name that has "worker" in it, [b]trait:hard_worker[/b] would look for pawn with actual Hard worker trait.

Everything is case-insensitive. Both [b]PROTEIN[/b] and [b]protein[/b] will look for pawn with unfortuname name Protein.

        [td][b]!passion:[/b], [b]!p:[/b][/td]

    [/tr]
    [tr]
        [td][b]trait:[/b], [b]t:[/b][/td]
        [td]Pawn has given trait. Search must be exact name. [b]trait:tough[/b] looks for a pawn with tough trait.[/td]
    [/tr]
    [tr]
        [td][b]!trait:[/b], [b]!t:[/b][/td]
        [td]Pawn has given trait. Search must be exact name. [b]!trait:Psychically_deaf[/b] looks for a pawns that are not psychically deaf.[/td]
    [/tr]
    [tr]
        [td][b]age>[/b], [b]a>[/b][/td]
        [td]Pawn is older than X. Search must be number. [b]age>12[/b] looks for a pawns are not children anymore.[/td]
    [/tr]
    [tr]
        [td][b]age<[/b], [b]a<[/b][/td]
        [td]Pawn is younger than X. Search must be number. [b]age<4[/b] looks for babies.[/td]
    [/tr]
    [tr]
        [td][b]hediff:[/b], [b]h:[/b][/td]
        [td]Pawn has health condition or implant. Search can be only part of hediff name. [b]hediff:pregnant[/b] looks for a pawns are expecting a child.[/td]
    [/tr]
    [tr]
        [td][b]!hediff:[/b], [b]!h:[/b][/td]
        [td]Pawn does not have health condition or implant. Search can be only part of hediff name. [b]!hediff:archotech_eye[/b] looks for pawns without archotech eye implant.[/td]
    [/tr]
    [tr]
        [td][b]ability:[/b], [b]ab:[/b][/td]
        [td]Pawn has ability. Search term can be only part of ability name. [b]ab:[/b] looks for .[/td]
    [/tr]
    [tr]
        [td][b]!ability:[/b], [b]!ab:[/b][/td]
        [td]Pawn does not have ability. SSearch term can be only part of ability name. [b]!abiliy:[/b] looks for .[/td]
    [/tr]
    [tr]
        [td]anything else[/td]
        [td]Part of pawns name.[/td]
    [/tr]    
[/table]

[h2]Mod compatibility[/h2] 

Will work with modded rituals.

Adding to existing save is safe. Removing from existing save is also safe.
